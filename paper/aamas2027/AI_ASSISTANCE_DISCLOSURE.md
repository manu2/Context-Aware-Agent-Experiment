# AI-assistance disclosure record for AAMAS 2027

This file is an internal preparation record. Before submission, convert the
relevant content into an anonymous supplementary disclosure consistent with the
AAMAS 2027 policy on AI-assisted technologies.

## Uses requiring disclosure

- AI assistants were used to brainstorm and adversarially review the research
  question, experimental design, validity controls, and manuscript organization.
- AI assistants were used to help implement and review harness code, tests,
  analysis scripts, and prose.
- Commercial language models are themselves the experimental subjects; their
  exact task prompts, recovery prompts, configured model identifiers, provider
  settings, raw responses, and generated programs are preserved in the frozen
  experiment archive.

## Author accountability

The human author selected the research question, approved the frozen protocol,
reviewed the code and evidence, authorized paid calls, and remains responsible for
the accuracy, originality, citations, statistical interpretation, and submitted
text. AI systems are not authors or co-authors.
