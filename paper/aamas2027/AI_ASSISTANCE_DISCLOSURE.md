# AI-assistance disclosure for AAMAS 2027

Generative-AI assistants supported brainstorming, adversarial review, code and test
implementation, evidence checking, and prose editing. The evaluated commercial
language models are also experimental subjects; their task prompts, recovery
prompts, configured model identifiers, generated responses, and programs are
preserved in the anonymous evidence package.

The human author formulated the research question, selected and froze the
experimental protocol, authorized data collection, reviewed the generated code
and execution evidence, verified the analysis and citations, and takes full
responsibility for the originality, accuracy, interpretation, and submitted text.
No AI system is an author or co-author.
